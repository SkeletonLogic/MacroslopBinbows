Patch Notes

Gameplay

  • NewJump jets for every 'Mech. All mech chassis can fit Jump Jets, but default variants are unchanged.
  • NewBonus salvage. Every 10,000 Resource Points left at the end of a mission earns one free 'Mech salvage.
  • Changed'Mech names loosened. Up to 22 characters, and punctuation such as ' : * . , < > is allowed.
  • FixedSound effects no longer change each other's volume when several weapons fire at once.

Graphics

  • NewModern renderer. The Direct3D 7 renderer is replaced with WebGPU (Direct3D 12 / Vulkan on desktop).
  • NewRender Distance setting, separate from fog.
  • NewCamera tilts to the horizon and shows the sky the game always had but never let you see.
  • ChangedWidescreen and high resolutions.
  • ChangedFrame rate uncapped.
